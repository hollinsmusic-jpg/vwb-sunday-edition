🔊 Audio Output Routing

Set this once based on your audio interface. Stereo sends everything to one output. Multi-Output uses a fixed 8-channel layout: Piano (Ch 1-2 stereo), Bass (Ch 3), Guitar (Ch 4), Rhythm Loop (Ch 5), Aux Stems (Ch 6-7 stereo), Q/Click Monitor (Ch 8 — monitor only, not sent to house).

↓ Fade Out

Controls how long the music takes to fade to silence when you press the Fade button on the Perform page. Works for all three engines — Audio, MIDI, and Organ.

Fade Duration
How many seconds the fade takes from full volume to silence.

⏱ Section Jump Timing

Controls how quickly VWB responds when you press a section button during playback. Jumping on a downbeat sounds most natural. Choose based on how much lead time you need.

Jump Quantize
When to fire the section jump after you press the button.

↔ Song Crossfade

Controls how VWB transitions between songs in the Service Flow. Set once and it applies to your whole service.

Transition Mode
How songs hand off to each other during a live service.
Fade Duration
How long the crossfade takes in seconds.
3s
Manual: song stops at end — you press Next when ready.  Auto: crossfade triggers when you press Next Cue.  Auto-Advance: songs crossfade automatically when they end — fully hands-free.
